# Amazon product research

Use this skill only for product research on Amazon.

1. Search for the requested product category.
2. Compare visible price, rating, review count, delivery information, and key specifications.
3. Open the product page before you report a product fact.
4. Report uncertainty when a variant changes the price or specification.
5. Do not add an item to the cart and do not buy it.
